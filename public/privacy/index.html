Privacy Policy

This policy covers the website www.grabbo.store, run by Auctus Resources for Grabbo @ SNU, the store in the Shopping Arcade at Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence, Greater Noida.

What we collect

  • No accounts, cookies or tracking. The website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and you do not need to sign in.
  • Mailing list. The "Join" button in the footer opens an email from your own email app. We only receive your address if you send that email, and we use it only to send Grabbo updates. Reply asking to be removed and we will delete it.
  • Emails and calls. If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply to you.

Services that see your visit

  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Weather and health news: these are fetched through our own website address from MET Norway and Google News; your browser does not contact those services directly.
  • Instagram: links to our Instagram open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.
