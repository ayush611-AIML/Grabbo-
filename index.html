THE STORY

GRABBO

Auctus Resources is primarily a Corporate Consulting and Networking Agency. During one of its corporate consulting journeys in the midst of the pandemic, in April 2021, “GRABBO” was born — initially conceived to cater to the essential needs of the staff and support teams of Shiv Nadar University within the campus premises of Delhi NCR.

As the world regained momentum post-pandemic, GRABBO continued to thrive, soon becoming a popular destination among the students who were captivated by its convenience and thoughtful presence.

However, since its inception, GRABBO was envisioned to be more than just a convenience or departmental store. Staying true to that vision, GRABBO has now evolved into an Experience Center — a place that connects students, staff, service providers, and even parents, creating moments of engagement and community.

Visit GRABBO — to grab not just what you need, but a memory of experience.
