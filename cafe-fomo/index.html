CAFÉ FOMO

“Feed Your FOMO”

From late-night study crunches to mid-day chill sessions, CAFÉ FOMO is Grabbo’s brand-new physical venture—an immersive campus hangout hub where artisanal brews, bold café bites, vibrant campus culture, and spontaneous conversations come alive. No rush, no routine. Just good energy and unforgettable campus memories.

Signature Highlights

Artisanal Brews & Cold Coffees

Small-batch pour overs, nitrogen cold brews, espresso tonics, and velvet flat whites crafted from single-origin beans.

🥐 Must Try

Loaded Café Bites

Crispy artisanal sourdough melts, warm flaky croissants, decadent cruffins, savory bagels, and handcrafted dips.

✨ Crowd Favorite

Instagrammable Hangout Vibe

Warm ambient lighting, comic accents, cozy communal corners, vinyl music beats, and aesthetic photo ops.

Exclusive Offline Drops

Surprise off-menu seasonal tastings, limited Grabbo x Café FOMO merch, live student acoustic nights, and pop-up events.
